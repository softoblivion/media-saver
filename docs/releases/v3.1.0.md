## ⪩ Media Saver v3.1.0 ⪨

A found update opens a sheet with its release notes, the source selector shows icons, every quality control has
four steps, the queue runs from the top, and previews arrive while the links are still waiting.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Update sheet** — when a newer version is found, a sheet opens over the window with the new version, the
  installed version, the size and the date of the release and its full release notes. **Install** downloads the
  update if it is not on disk yet and restarts the app as soon as the file is verified; **Later** closes the sheet
  until you click the version label next to the app name. With **Download updates automatically** on, the sheet
  shows the download progress and then **Restart to update**.
- **Quality for MP3** — the quality control now applies to MP3 too: Low 128 kbps, Medium 192 kbps, High 256 kbps,
  Max 320 kbps.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **Source selector** — Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and X are icons in the middle of the title bar; the selected
  one is lit in the theme's accent. The names stay as tooltips.
- **Quality** — four steps for every source: Low (up to 480p), Medium (up to 720p), High (up to 1080p) and Max
  (the best the video has; YouTube up to 4K). A saved YouTube setting of 360p or 720p becomes Low or Medium.
- **Queue order** — downloads start from the top of the list. A pasted batch is listed in the order you pasted it
  and runs top to bottom; a link pasted later appears above and starts next.
- **Previews** — thumbnails, titles and durations of queued links are fetched three at a time, top of the list
  first, and show up while the links are still waiting; a card keeps its placeholder until its preview arrives, also
  when the download has already started.
- **Version label** — the version pill sits right of the app name in the title bar; its panel and the update
  behaviour are unchanged.
- **Fixed window size** — the window is 1180 x 780 (smaller steps on a small screen) and can no longer be resized
  or maximized; the maximize button and the double click on the title bar are gone.

### ⤷ Removed ˎˊ˗

- The `Ctrl+Enter` shortcut for Download and its hint under the button. Click **Download**.
- The "new version available" toast; the sheet is the notice. The "ready to install" toast still appears when a
  background download finishes while the sheet is closed.
