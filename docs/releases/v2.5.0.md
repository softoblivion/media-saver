## ⪩ Media Saver v2.5.0 ⪨

The version label, the update panel and the update settings now use the shared interface kit of the author's
desktop apps; updating works as before.

---

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **Version pill and update panel** — the version in the title bar is a small glass pill with a dot when a new
  version is available, downloading (pulsing) or ready (mint). Its panel shows the installed and the latest version,
  the release notes with links that open in the browser, the download progress, and one action: **Check for
  updates**, **Download**, **Restart to update** or **Try again**. Opening the panel refreshes the status.
- **Updates in Settings** — a new **Updates** group shows the update status with its next action (check, download,
  the download percentage or restart), **Check interval** and **Download updates automatically**; it replaces the
  update rows of the About group.
- **Background and controls** — the background adds slowly rising specks and a soft light sweep in the same colours;
  switches, steppers and menus follow the shared kit's sizes. Fonts are unchanged.
