## ⪩ Media Saver v2.3.0 ⪨

Updates download in the background and wait for one click; a sound marks each finished download.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Background update download** — a new version found at launch, or by the check that repeats every 6 hours while the app runs, downloads in the background and is verified as before. A notice "Media Saver X.Y.Z is ready" offers **Restart**; the app never restarts by itself. Switch: Settings > **Download updates automatically** (on by default).
- **Finish sound** — a short sound when a download finishes (once for a batch that finishes together). Switch: Settings > **Sound when a download finishes** (on by default).

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- Shorter, plain texts in the link panel and the empty queue (for example "Clean links appear here as `youtube.com/watch?v=id`").
- The **Download** label is centred in its button.

### ⤷ Fixed ˎˊ˗

- The `Ctrl+Enter` hint no longer pushes the button text off centre when it is hidden.

### ⤷ Removed ˎˊ˗

- **Install updates automatically** (automatic restart) and its `auto_install_updates` setting, replaced by the background download above.
