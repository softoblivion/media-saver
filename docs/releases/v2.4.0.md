## ⪩ Media Saver v2.4.0 ⪨

New versions are found within 30 minutes while the app runs, and the check interval is a setting.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Update check interval** — the app looks for a new version on launch and then every 30 minutes while it runs
  (before: every 6 hours). Settings > About > **Update check**: Every 30 minutes, Every hour, Every 6 hours, Every
  day or Every week. A change applies at once; a found version still downloads in the background when **Download
  updates automatically** is on, and installing is still a click on **Restart**.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- The `Ctrl+Enter` hint moved out of the **Download** button into a small muted line under it, shown while links
  are ready to download. The button label no longer shares its space with the hint.
