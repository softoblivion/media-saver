## ⪩ Media Saver v2.1.2 ⪨

Checking for updates and installing them are now two separate things.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Install updates automatically** — a switch in Settings (off by default). When on, a new version found at
  launch is downloaded in the background and installed as soon as the queue is idle, with a short notice before
  the restart.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **The update check always runs** on launch and shows the new version in the window; nothing is downloaded or
  installed without your click unless the new switch is on. The former "Check for updates on launch" switch is
  gone.

### ⤷ Fixed ˎˊ˗

- Nothing.

### ⤷ Removed ˎˊ˗

- The `check_app_updates` setting (ignored when present in an older settings file).
