## ⪩ Media Saver v3.3.1 ⪨

The update sheet keeps its buttons in view with long release notes, and a future major version is offered as a choice instead of an automatic update.

---

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **New major versions** — a release with a new major version (for example 4.0) is no longer downloaded in the background. Its sheet explains that it is a new major version, shows its release notes and offers **Switch to 4.0** or **Stay on 3.x**. Stay keeps the current version and stops the notice for that major version; clicking the version pill in the title bar still offers the switch. Updates within the current major version work as before.

### ⤷ Fixed ˎˊ˗

- **Update sheet with long release notes** — when the release notes were taller than the window, they did not scroll and **Later** and **Install** were cut off below the sheet. The notes now scroll inside the sheet, and the buttons and an error message stay visible at every window size.
