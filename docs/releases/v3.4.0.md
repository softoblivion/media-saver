## ⪩ Media Saver v3.4.0 ⪨

A resizable window that fits every screen, a queue that recovers from closed apps, failed and long downloads, and every new version, a new major version included, installed like any other update.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Resizable window** — drag any edge or corner, down to a minimum size. A taller window shows more of the queue, a wider one widens the two panels up to a comfortable width. The window never maximizes; a drag to the left or right edge of the screen still snaps it to that half.
- **Remembered place and size** — the window opens where it was closed, at the same size. If that screen is no longer connected, it opens in the middle of the main screen.
- **Resume interrupted downloads** — downloads stopped by closing the app are listed in a row at the top of the queue at the next start; **Resume** starts the interrupted downloads of the platform shown, and nothing starts without the click.
- **Retry failed** — a button in the queue header, shown while the list has failed downloads, starts every failed download of the current platform again.
- **Download again** — when a finished file was moved or deleted, the "File not found" notice offers to download it again.
- **Stalled downloads** — a download that receives no data shows how long it has waited and when it will be stopped.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **No one-hour limit** — downloads and conversions are no longer stopped after one hour. A download is stopped after 5 minutes without data and a conversion after 10 minutes without progress, with a message that names which of the two stalled; a slow download that keeps receiving data runs to the end.
- **Scale per screen** — changing Scale grows or shrinks the window around its centre. On each screen the window uses the largest Scale step that fits it, and Settings dims the steps that do not fit and shows the step in use; the chosen step is kept and comes back on a screen large enough for it.
- **Windows that leave the screen** — after a move to another screen, a change of display scale, resolution or taskbar, a window that no longer fits is moved and shrunk into the screen, so its title bar buttons stay reachable. Moving between screens with different display scales keeps the window's size and a sharp page.
- **Queue counts** — the queue header counts cancelled and interrupted downloads as "stopped" instead of "failed", so "failed" matches what **Retry failed** starts again.
- **New major versions** — a release with a new major version (for example 4.0) is an ordinary update: it is downloaded in the background when **Download updates automatically** is on and installed with **Restart to update**. The choice to stay on the current major version is gone.
- **Link counting** — two links to the same video (a Shorts and a watch link, a post and a Reel link, two forms of one X post) count as one link in the preview.

### ⤷ Fixed ˎˊ˗

- A TikTok short link and the full link of the same video no longer download the video twice.
- A download retried just before closing the app is no longer restored as failed.
- The download history can no longer be overwritten by an older state when several downloads finish together.
- A download held while the download engine updates itself shows "Waiting for engine update" on its card.
- A download history file that cannot be read is kept as `history.json.bad` in the data folder instead of being replaced.
- An update that is downloaded and ready to install stays ready when a later update check finds no network.
